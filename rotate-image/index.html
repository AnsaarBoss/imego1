About Imego Image Rotator

The Imego Image Rotator lets you correct photo orientations and flip images horizontally or vertically. Smartphone cameras frequently tag photos with incorrect orientation metadata, causing pictures to display sideways or upside-down when uploaded to websites, PDF documents, or online stores. Our tool rotates every pixel to correct orientation permanently.

Rotating vs. Mirroring / Flipping

Understanding the visual difference between rotation and mirror flipping ensures proper adjustments:

  • 90° / 180° Rotation: Turns the canvas clockwise or counter-clockwise to correct sideways portraits or landscape camera angles without altering left-to-right spatial order.
  • Horizontal Flip (Mirror): Reverses the image left-to-right. Useful for fixing front-facing selfie video frames, adjusting subject direction toward text columns, or creating artistic reflection effects.
  • Vertical Flip: Inverts the canvas upside-down, useful for correcting inverted scanner outputs or technical graphic feeds.

Practical Usage Tips

When flipping selfies or scanned paperwork containing readable text, verify that words and logo branding remain legible. Rotating photos directly inside your browser rewrites image pixels cleanly without loss of quality or unexpected orientation flips on external device viewports.
