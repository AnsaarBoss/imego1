Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

At Imego we ara accessible from https://ansaarboss.github.io/imego1/, protecting your privacy is one of our primary commitments. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the measures we take to safeguard your data.

1. Client-Side Image Processing & File Privacy

All image manipulation operations (including compression, resizing, converting, cropping, rotating, upscaling, favicon generation, and EXIF inspection) are performed 100% locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas technologies.

Your uploaded photos and graphics are never uploaded, transferred, or stored on any external server or cloud service. Your files remain exclusively on your device throughout the entire processing workflow and are immediately cleared when you close or refresh your browser tab.

2. Analytics & Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party services to help us analyze website traffic and display relevant advertisements that support the ongoing maintenance and free availability of our tools:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior details (such as pages visited, time spent on site, device browser types, and approximate geographic region). This aggregated data helps us understand how users interact with Imego and improve site performance.
  • Google AdSense & Third-Party Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. For complete details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google’s Advertising Policies & Privacy Terms.

3. Local Storage Usage

Imego uses your browser’s Local Storage (`localStorage`) solely to store your website display preferences—specifically, whether you have enabled Light Mode or Dark Mode. This ensures your visual color theme preference persists across browser sessions. We do not store personal details, image metrics, or user tracking tokens in local storage.

4. Cookie Consent & Disabling Cookies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a web page server. You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your individual browser settings. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer.

Please note that disabling cookies in your browser settings may affect the display of personalized advertisements, but it will not impact your ability to use any of Imego's image processing utilities.

5. Children's Privacy Information

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important to us. Imego is a general audience tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will make best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational practices, legal obligations, or service offerings. We will notify users of any modifications by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically for any updates.
