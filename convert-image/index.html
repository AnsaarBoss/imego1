Choosing the Right Format: JPG vs PNG vs WebP

Selecting the ideal image extension depends on the visual content of your file:

  • JPG / JPEG: Best for real-world photography, nature shots, and complex gradients. JPG does not support transparent backgrounds, filling empty pixels with white.
  • PNG: Essential for graphics requiring transparent backgrounds, corporate brand logos, app icons, and UI screenshots containing readable vector typography.
  • WebP: The modern web standard backed by Google. WebP combines PNG's full transparency support with JPG's compact file sizing, reducing loading times significantly across modern desktop and mobile browsers.

When to Convert Your Files

Convert heavy PNG photos to JPG or WebP before uploading them to website CMS platforms to boost search engine SEO scores. Convert JPG logos back into PNG format when you need clean transparency layers for overlaying artwork over colored page sections.
