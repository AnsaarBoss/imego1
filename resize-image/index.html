About Imego Image Resizer

The Imego Image Resizer allows you to modify the exact pixel dimensions (width and height) of your digital photos and illustrations. Modern digital cameras and smartphones take massive photos that often exceed 4000x3000 pixels. Publishing raw dimensions on blogs or social media wastes bandwidth and slows page rendering. Resizing scales these dimensions down to exact target specifications in seconds.

Recommended Dimensions for Common Uses

Matching your canvas size to its final destination ensures photos render clearly without awkward stretching:

  • Website Banners & Hero Headers: 1920x1080 pixels (16:9 full HD ratio).
  • Blog Content Images: 1200x630 pixels (optimized for article body alignment and social link shares).
  • Social Media Posts: 1080x1080 pixels for square Instagram feeds or 1080x1350 pixels for vertical posts.
  • E-Commerce Product Display: 1000x1000 pixels on white background canvases.

Maintaining Aspect Ratio

Always keep the aspect ratio lock enabled when resizing human portraits, products, or brand typography. Constraining proportions prevents subjects from looking squished or unnaturally stretched. Downscaling high-resolution photos also sharpens image fidelity, making your graphics crisp on retina and high-DPI displays.
